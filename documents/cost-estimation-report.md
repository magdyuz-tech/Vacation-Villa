# Cost Estimation Report

**22 measured items. No total.**

This report is class **X**: it cannot be completed here. The quantities are ours and exact; the rates are not ours at all, and an elemental estimate is quantities times rates.

What follows is therefore the estimate with every quantity filled in and every rate left open, which is the honest shape of it. Filling the rate column from memory or from another country's prices would produce a number, and the number would be believed.

## What is needed to complete it

1. Current rates for Menoufia, from a named source — a local quantity surveyor, a recent comparable build, or quotations.
2. The structural design, which fixes the concrete and reinforcement that are the largest single element in a frame like this and are not measurable yet (STR series, class L).
3. The services design, same reason (EL, ME and PL series).
4. The specification, which decides what the finishes are (QS-003).
5. Preliminaries, overheads, profit, contingency and professional fees, none of which is a quantity.

## Excluded

1. Everything below ground. The foundation type is not chosen and its depth is not known; GEO-001 and STR-101 come first.
2. Slab, beam and footing thicknesses, so no concrete volume is given for them. The areas are ours; the depths are the structural engineer's, and a volume needs both.
3. Reinforcement, in any form.
4. Services. The EL, ME and PL series are coordination layouts; their quantities follow the services engineer's design.
5. Rates, overheads, preliminaries, profit and contingency.
