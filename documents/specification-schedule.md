# Material Requisitions and Specification Schedule

What has to be decided before the measure can be bought.

These are topics, not specifications. Naming a block strength or a membrane here would be inventing the one thing this document exists to ask for — it is issued so that whoever specifies has a complete list to answer, and so that nothing is priced against an assumption nobody wrote down.

| Trade | To be specified |
| --- | --- |
| Masonry | Both walls are brick, specified by the client: the 100 mm partitions red, the 300 mm external and party walls any colour. Still to be specified: brick size and strength, the parapet's material, mortar, movement joints, wall ties and lintels over every opening. |
| Concrete | Grade, cover, aggregate and finish — all to the structural engineer's specification, not this one. |
| Openings | Frame material and finish, glazing build-up and performance, operation, ironmongery, seals and thresholds. ARC-500 measures the openings; none of this is in them. |
| Stair | Tread and riser finish, nosing profile and its slip resistance, the balustrade's infill and fixings, the handrail arrangement — one side or both, wall-fixed or on the balustrade, and whether the code in force requires extensions past the top and bottom risers. Flights, landings and the balustrade are measured in QS-001; the handrail is named there and left unmeasured until this is answered. |
| Internal finishes | Plaster system, paint, which rooms are tiled and to what height, floor finish per room, skirtings and junctions. |
| External finishes | Render system and colour, the slab nosing and its cove light, balustrade glass and fixings. |
| Roof | Membrane, insulation, falls screed, upstands, outlets and overflows, protection and the walking surface. The plant deck at the north-west carries the water tanks: their number, capacity, material, supports and the pipework to them are all to be specified, and how anyone reaches the deck is an open design item rather than a specification one — the stair stops at the penthouse. |
| External works | Paving material and bedding, sub-base, falls, edgings, turf and topsoil, irrigation, the gate, the street wall's coping and finish. The pergola's timber, its section, fixing and preservative treatment, and the barbecue counter's structure and worktop. |
| Services | Everything. The EL, ME and PL series are coordination only. |

Quantities for every item above are in QS-001 and QS-002 where the geometry fixes them, and are marked class L or X where it does not.
