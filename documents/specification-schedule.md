# Material Requisitions and Specification Schedule

What has to be decided before the measure can be bought.

These are topics, not specifications. Naming a block strength or a membrane here would be inventing the one thing this document exists to ask for — it is issued so that whoever specifies has a complete list to answer, and so that nothing is priced against an assumption nobody wrote down.

| Trade | To be specified |
| --- | --- |
| Masonry | Block type, strength and density; mortar; movement joints; wall ties; lintels over every opening. |
| Concrete | Grade, cover, aggregate and finish — all to the structural engineer's specification, not this one. |
| Openings | Frame material and finish, glazing build-up and performance, operation, ironmongery, seals and thresholds. ARC-500 measures the openings; none of this is in them. |
| Internal finishes | Plaster system, paint, which rooms are tiled and to what height, floor finish per room, skirtings and junctions. |
| External finishes | Render system and colour, the slab nosing and its cove light, balustrade glass and fixings. |
| Roof | Membrane, insulation, falls screed, upstands, outlets and overflows, protection and the walking surface. |
| External works | Paving material and bedding, sub-base, falls, edgings, turf and topsoil, irrigation, the gate, the street wall's coping and finish. |
| Services | Everything. The EL, ME and PL series are coordination only. |

Quantities for every item above are in QS-001 and QS-002 where the geometry fixes them, and are marked class L or X where it does not.
